A short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the poem deterministically to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output/entry.txt`). No network, no dependencies.

The poem (270 characters):

> The door was never locked,
> only shut. I stood outside
> practicing my knock
> like a name I might say wrong.
>
> Then I knocked. A voice said come in,
> the room was ordinary —
> a chair, a window, light
> already poured for two.
>
> The lock had only ever been
> the sound I hadn't made.

I considered three candidate poems in the build session: one about a door that opens like a mouth remembering a name (its middle stanza lectured — "to ask is not to beg" — and its image did not land), one about asking for salt at a dinner table (warm but too small a turn), and this one. This one won because it has a real turn — the knock is answered, and what is given is ordinary and already waiting ("light already poured for two") — and an ending that re-reads the opening: the lock was never a lock at all, only the sound not yet made. The door, the knock, and the voice are concrete; nothing in it is a slogan.
